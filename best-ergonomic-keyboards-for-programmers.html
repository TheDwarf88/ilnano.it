As a programmer, spending hours upon hours writing code can take a serious toll on your hands, wrists, and shoulders. Repetitive strain injuries (RSI) are a very real hazard in our profession, making the gear you use every single day a crucial investment in your long-term health. A standard, flat keyboard forces your wrists into unnatural angles, leading to fatigue and discomfort by the end of a long debugging session. Ergonomic keyboards are specifically engineered to keep your hands in a more neutral, relaxed posture. Whether you prefer a split design, a curved layout, or integrated cushioned palm rests, upgrading your workstation with the right ergonomic keyboard can drastically improve your typing endurance, speed, and overall comfort. We have tested and reviewed the top options available on the market today to help you find the perfect match for your coding workflow.
